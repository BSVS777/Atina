**Administrador has access to everything (professor-confirmed, 2026-08-24 — see `Docs/DIARIO_DECISIONES_IA.md`).** All Academic policies are gated on permissions, never hardcoded role names, and `RoleSeeder::OFFICIAL_ROLE_PERMISSIONS['Administrador']` already grants all 16 official business permissions — including every one listed above plus `usuarios.gestionar` (teacher creation) and `atestados.gestionar` (credentials) — so Administrador can perform every Academic operation without needing the separate `Superadmin` `Gate::before` bypass. No production-code change: `AdministradorFullAccessTest` seeds the real `PermissionSeeder`/`RoleSeeder` and asserts the seeded "Administrador" role against every Academic policy ability (teacher creation, credentials, catalog, assignment proposal, no-catalog decision, Technical Note create/approve, exports).
